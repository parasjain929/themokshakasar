The Kasar Devi Experience

Life moves slower
at this altitude

Perched at 2,100 metres above sea level in the ancient Kumaon hills, Kasar Devi is one of those rare places where the earth seems to breathe differently. Physicists call it the Van Allen Belt — a zone of charged cosmic particles that has drawn seekers, artists, and wanderers for centuries.

At Moksha Retreat, we have built around this energy — stone walls, deodar wood beams, handwoven textiles, and windows that frame the Himalayan panorama like living paintings. This is not a place to rush through. Stay a week. Maybe two.

For the Remote Wanderer

Workation at Altitude

Kasar Devi has long attracted writers, musicians, and thinkers. Now it's calling remote workers too. Fast fibre WiFi, a dedicated co-working lounge with panoramic Himalayan views, and a culture that celebrates deep focus.

We offer month-long retreat packages with ergonomic workspaces, unlimited filter coffee, curated evening experiences, and zero interruptions — except the occasional Himalayan crow who refuses to be ignored.
