Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before you arrive.

From Delhi: Take an overnight train to Kathgodam (Shatabdi / Ranikhet Express), then a 3-hour cab/shared taxi to Kasar Devi village, which sits 7km above the town of Almora. The full journey from Delhi takes approximately 10-12 hours door to door. We can arrange a private cab from Kathgodam. WhatsApp us for pricing and availability.

From Mumbai: Fly to Pantnagar airport (1.5 hrs), then cab to Moksha (approximately 3.5 hours). Alternatively, fly to Delhi and take the overnight train.

If you're driving, the Google Maps route via NH9 from Delhi through Rampur is the most scenic — approximately 350km, 8-9 hours.

Kasar Devi is beautiful year-round, but each season offers a distinct experience:

Spring (March–May): Rhododendrons in bloom, clear mountain views, mild temperatures. Peak season for the retreat.

Monsoon (July–September): Dramatic clouds and mist, lush green forest, occasional rain. The most atmospheric time. Some treks become inaccessible.

Autumn (October–November): Crystal-clear mountain views, golden light, harvest season. Many guests' favourite time.

Winter (December–February): Cold nights (0°C to -5°C), occasional snowfall, fireplaces lit every evening. Magical and very quiet.

We welcome children above the age of 7. The property has outdoor spaces, a garden, and is surrounded by safe forest trails. Children generally have a wonderful time here — collecting pine cones, watching Himalayan birds, and helping in the herb garden.

However, please note that Moksha is designed as a mindful, quiet retreat. We ask that families be mindful of quiet hours (10 PM to 7 AM) and the meditative atmosphere we cultivate, especially in the yoga and wellness spaces.

If you're travelling with very young children (under 7), please contact us first so we can discuss whether the property is a right fit for your family's needs.

Our kitchen is primarily vegetarian — rooted in the Kumaoni hill tradition of wholesome, plant-forward cooking. Our breakfast is fully vegetarian and includes local specialties: mandua (finger millet) rotis, bhatt ki churkani (black soybean dal), seasonal preserves, local honey, and fresh chai.

For dinner (available as an add-on), we can accommodate vegan, gluten-free, and most allergy-related dietary requirements with advance notice. Please mention your requirements when making a booking inquiry.

We do not serve beef or pork at the property. Eggs are available on request. We are happy to make eggs in any style for breakfast.
