About Us

Experience Serenity at Moksha

Nestled on the scenic Kasar–Binsar Road, Moksha Retreat is a peaceful mountain sanctuary surrounded by pine forests and breathtaking Himalayan landscapes.

The property offers stunning 360-degree panoramic views along with uninterrupted sightlines to the majestic Himalayan ranges. Designed for travellers seeking comfort, nature, and tranquility, Moksha features 17 thoughtfully designed rooms across multiple categories — catering to couples, families, solo travellers, and groups alike.

Every corner of the property is crafted to blend modern comfort with the calm essence of the mountains. Whether you are planning a peaceful getaway, a wellness retreat, a workation, or a mountain vacation with friends and family — Moksha offers the perfect balance of comfort, nature, and soulful Himalayan living.

At the Property

Everything You Need, Nothing You Don't

Our in-house restaurant features a traditional tandoor setup serving warm, delicious meals with mesmerizing valley views — the kind of breakfast that makes you want to linger.

Unwind in the swimming pool, relax amidst the pines, and enjoy spacious communal areas ideal for yoga retreats, group gatherings, and recreational activities.
